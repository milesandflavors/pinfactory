<h2 id="2026-parade">The 2026 Parade: Confirmed Date and Expected Route</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade takes place on Thursday, November 26, 2026. The parade began in 1924 and paused from 1942 through 1944 during World War II, so 2026 is the 100th individual parade rather than its 100th anniversary.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Macy's had not released the final 2026 route when this guide was updated in August. Recent editions have started near West 77th Street and Central Park West, continued toward Columbus Circle and down Sixth Avenue, and finished at Macy's Herald Square. Treat that route as an early planning reference and confirm it on the <a href="https://www.macys.com/s/parade/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">official parade page</a> before choosing a hotel or viewing spot.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 id="where-to-watch">Best Places to Watch in Person</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Do not commit to a viewing block until Macy's publishes its official 2026 spectator map. In recent editions, designated stretches of <strong>Central Park West</strong> and <strong>Sixth Avenue</strong> have offered public viewing. The exact turn at <strong>Columbus Circle</strong>, however, has previously been closed to public viewing, so do not build your plan around standing there.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The television production zone around <strong>34th to 38th Street</strong> has also been restricted in previous years, and <strong>Herald Square</strong> is not a dependable public-viewing choice. Once the 2026 map is live, choose a clearly designated public section farther uptown instead of assuming the finish area will be accessible.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For recent parades, spectators aiming for a front-row position have arrived around 6 a.m. Expect security controls, road closures, and dense crowds, but verify the final 2026 access guidance before deciding when and where to arrive.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 id="balloon-inflation">Watching the Balloon Inflation the Night Before</h2>

<p>The balloon inflation has traditionally taken place near the American Museum of Natural History on the day before Thanksgiving. If Macy's opens it to the public again, the expected date would be <strong>Wednesday, November 25, 2026</strong>. It can be a better fit than parade morning if you want to see the balloons up close without spending most of Thanksgiving at one viewing spot.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The exact 2026 hours, entrance, security rules, and public-access details were not yet confirmed when this guide was updated. Recent editions have used a controlled entrance near West 72nd Street and Columbus Avenue, but check the official Macy's announcement before traveling across the city. If access is confirmed, arrive early and expect substantial crowds.</p>

<h2 id="tv-streaming">Watching on TV or Streaming</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>NBCUniversal has confirmed coverage of the 100th parade, but the final 2026 airtime, Peacock availability, and any encore schedule were not yet posted when this guide was updated. Recent editions have aired from <strong>8:30 a.m. to noon</strong> across U.S. time zones. Check NBC or Peacock closer to Thanksgiving before planning your morning around that schedule.</p>

<h2 id="what-to-bring">What to Bring If You're Watching In Person</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Late November in New York can be cold, and you may be standing outside for hours before the parade reaches your block. Dress in warm layers, wear comfortable shoes, and bring hand warmers if you tend to feel the cold.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A portable phone charger is useful because cold weather and frequent photos can drain your battery quickly. Bathroom access is limited, and subway stations near the route can be extremely crowded before and after the parade, so build extra time into both journeys.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Review the official 2026 security guidance before leaving your hotel. Previous editions have restricted items such as folding chairs, umbrellas, backpacks, and large bags, but the current rules should be your final reference.</p>
