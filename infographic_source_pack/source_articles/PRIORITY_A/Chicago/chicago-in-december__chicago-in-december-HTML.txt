<p>December in Chicago means real cold — wind chill off Lake Michigan is not a myth, and it's the coldest month you'll find on this site's Chicago calendar so far. It's also when the city commits fully to the season: mulled wine steaming in the cold air, lights strung over the river, and Christkindlmarket in full swing at Daley Plaza. Whether the month is worth the layers depends on which week you're actually talking about.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="how-id-plan-december">How I'd plan a December trip to Chicago</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Decide first whether you want the holiday spectacle or a quieter, cheaper city trip — December delivers very different versions of Chicago depending on the week. Early December, before the week of Christmas, still has Christkindlmarket and the lights running, but without the peak-week crowds and prices. I'd target this window if I wanted the atmosphere without paying the Christmas-week premium.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If the full spectacle is the point, I'd shortlist the <a href="https://www.viator.com/tours/Chicago/Chicago-Holiday-Lights-Tour/d673-5765CHIGHTS?pid=P00300034&amp;mcid=42383&amp;medium=link&amp;campaign=chicago-in-december" target="_blank" rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer">Chicago Holiday Lights Tour on Viator</a> — an organized evening route is easier than connecting multiple lit-up areas yourself once it's dark by 4:30pm and genuinely cold outside.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="weather">Weather in Chicago in December — What to Pack</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://www.weather.gov/lot/chicago_normals" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">National Weather Service climate normals for Chicago O'Hare</a> put December's average high at 36.6°F and average low at 24.4°F (2.6°C and -4.2°C), with colder spells genuinely possible. Wind chill depends on both the actual temperature and wind speed, so check the hourly "feels like" forecast rather than trusting a fixed rule.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is a full winter-coat month, not a jacket month. Pack an insulated coat, thermal base layers, waterproof footwear with real grip, a hat that covers your ears, a scarf, and touchscreen gloves. Sidewalks can be wet, salted, icy, or slushy depending on the day, so grip matters more than looks.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="does-it-snow">Does It Snow in Chicago in December?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Yes, snow is a normal part of a Chicago December, though it's rarely the heavy, trip-disrupting kind for most of the month. Expect a mix of cold, dry days and days with light-to-moderate snow rather than a guaranteed white-Christmas scene — some years deliver it, some don't. Either way, plan footwear and outdoor time around the possibility rather than assuming clear sidewalks.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="early-vs-holiday">Early December vs. Christmas Week vs. New Year's</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Early December</strong>, roughly the first two weeks, is the sweet spot: Christkindlmarket and the Magnificent Mile lights are both running, but Christmas-week crowds and prices haven't arrived yet. This is the window I'd pick for the atmosphere without the premium.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Christmas week</strong> itself is Chicago at its most crowded and most expensive — hotel rates climb, restaurants need reservations further ahead, and the core holiday sights get genuinely busy. It's also, unsurprisingly, when the city looks and feels its most festive, so it's a fair trade if the spectacle matters more than the crowds to you.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>New Year's</strong> brings its own spike — fireworks and events downtown push hotel demand right back up after a brief lull following Christmas Day. If you want a quieter stretch, the days between Christmas and New Year's Eve itself tend to be calmer than either peak surrounding them.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="events">Best Events in Chicago in December</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Christkindlmarket</strong> at Daley Plaza is the anchor of the month — free general admission, with mulled wine, ornaments, and gifts filling out the spending side. <strong>Millennium Park's ice rink</strong> has traditionally offered free admission with an online reservation, skate rental extra. <strong>ZooLights</strong> at Lincoln Park Zoo runs paid evenings through most of December, with select free Mondays. For the full breakdown of what's free and what's worth paying for across all of these, see <a href="/chicago-at-christmas/">Chicago at Christmas: What Is Actually Worth It</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="romantic">Romantic Things to Do in Chicago in Winter</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>December's cold has a genuine upside for couples: fewer crowds at the spots that get overrun in summer, and a specific kind of atmosphere that only shows up once it's actually cold. An evening walk along the Chicago Riverwalk with the bridges and skyline lights reflecting off the water is a low-effort, high-payoff pairing — free, and best after dark. Millennium Park's rink works as a low-stakes date if neither of you is a serious skater. For dinner, look toward River North rather than trying to fit a reservation into an already-packed Loop itinerary; it's close enough to walk from most downtown hotels.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="hotels">Hotel Prices and Crowds</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Hotel rates in Chicago follow the crowd pattern closely: lowest in early December, climbing through the second half of the month, and peaking around Christmas week and New Year's. If budget matters more than hitting the absolute peak of the decorations, book an early-December date. If the full spectacle is the priority, budget for Christmas-week pricing and book well ahead — good rooms in the Loop and River North sell out before the date gets close.</p>
