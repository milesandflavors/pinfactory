<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>October in Chicago is the month the city stops fighting the weather and starts working with it. The humidity that makes July feel heavy is gone, the lakefront path is finally comfortable for a full afternoon, and the trees in Millennium Park and along the river turn color right as the summer tourist crowd thins out.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It's also Marathon month, Halloween-parade month, and for one specific weekend, one of the toughest hotel weekends of the entire fall. Here's what actually changes in Chicago in October, and how to plan around it.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="weather">Weather in Chicago in October — What to Pack</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>October is a real transition month, and the swing from the start of the month to the end is bigger than people expect. National Weather Service climate normals for Chicago show average highs near 69°F and lows near 51°F on October 1, sliding to about 56°F and 40°F by October 31.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The full-month average is about 63°F / 45°F, with roughly 3.4 inches of precipitation. Translation: this is not heavy-coat winter yet, but it is absolutely layer weather, especially near Lake Michigan after sunset.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Pack in actual layers rather than one October "outfit" — a t-shirt and light jacket can work for an early-month afternoon, while a scarf and real jacket become necessary for a late-October evening on the lakefront.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="events">Best Events in Chicago in October</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"className":"wp-block-list"} -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong><a href="https://www.chicagomarathon.com/" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Chicago Marathon</a></strong> (October 11, 2026) — one of the World Marathon Majors, and the single biggest driver of hotel demand in the month. Confirm the exact date before booking, whether you're running it or just want to avoid the crowds and price spike.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong><a href="https://www.chicagofilmfestival.com/festival/" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Chicago International Film Festival</a></strong> (October 14-25, 2026) — North America's longest-running competitive film festival, with screenings and special events across the city.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong><a href="https://www.lincolnsquare.org/apple-fest" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Lincoln Square Apple Fest</a></strong> (October 2-4, 2026) — a neighborhood harvest festival with apple pie, cider, vendors, and fall-themed goods, a good low-key add if you're staying north side.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong><a href="https://www.chicagobotanic.org/halloween" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Night of 1,000 Jack-o'-Lanterns</a></strong> (October 7-25, 2026) — the Chicago Botanic Garden lights up with artist-carved pumpkins after dark; book ahead if you want a weekend or early-evening slot.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong><a href="https://www.architecture.org/open-house-chicago/about" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Open House Chicago</a></strong> (October 17-18, 2026) — a free citywide architecture weekend with behind-the-scenes access to significant buildings, cultural spaces, and neighborhood sites.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Halloween parades</strong> (late October) — <a href="https://artsinthedark.com/" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Arts in the Dark</a> on October 24, 2026, and the <a href="https://chicagoevents.com/event/haunted-halsted-halloween-parade/" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Northalsted/Haunted Halsted Halloween Parade</a> on October 31, one of the city's biggest costume-contest nights.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="foliage">Fall Foliage — Where to See It</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Chicago doesn't have the dramatic mountain foliage of New England, but the city's parks and lakefront still turn real color through October, especially in the back half of the month. <a href="/millennium-park-cloud-gate-chicago/">Millennium Park</a> and the trees around Cloud Gate are an easy, central spot to catch it without leaving downtown, and the <a href="/chicago-riverwalk-free-things-to-do/">Chicago Riverwalk</a> gets a similar seasonal change along its full 1.25-mile stretch — free to walk either way, and worth combining into the same afternoon.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="hotels">Hotel Prices and Crowds</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Outside Marathon weekend, October generally sits below summer peak pricing and ahead of the holiday-season climb that starts building toward Thanksgiving and the Christkindlmarket in November. That makes most of the month a genuinely good value window — fewer summer tourists, milder crowds at major sights, and rates that reflect it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The one exception is real: Chicago Marathon weekend can make downtown availability tight, and prices often rise to match. If your trip doesn't need to overlap with the race, checking the date and shifting by even a few days can mean a meaningfully different hotel budget.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-to-do">What to Do — October-Specific Picks</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Most of Chicago's essential sights work fine any month — see the <a href="/chicago-bucket-list-25-best-things-to-do/">Chicago bucket list guide</a> for the full list — but a few things specifically suit October. A night cruise takes on a different mood once there's a chill in the air and the skyline lights come up earlier in the evening. The <a href="/chicago-architecture-river-cruise/">Chicago Architecture River Cruise</a> is still worth doing for the buildings themselves, but for something built around the season, the Ghost &amp; Dark History cruise leans into October rather than working around it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you're traveling with kids, October adds a genuine seasonal layer to the usual stops — pumpkin patches and Halloween pop-ups show up at parks across the city through the month, on top of the normal family-friendly sights.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Pack real layers:</strong> a 20-degree swing between an early and late October day is normal, and evenings are consistently colder than afternoons.</li>

<li><strong>Book Halloween events ahead:</strong> Night of 1,000 Jack-o'-Lanterns and similar ticketed seasonal events sell out specific dates, especially weekends.</li>

<li><strong>Bring a light rain layer:</strong> October averages about 3.4 inches of precipitation, and a packable waterproof layer is more useful than an umbrella on a windy lakefront.</li>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="faq">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<!-- wp:details -->
<details class="wp-block-details"><summary>Is October a good time to visit Chicago?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Yes, generally. Weather is mild to cool, crowds are thinner than summer, and hotel prices are usually below peak — with the exception of Chicago Marathon weekend in mid-October, when downtown availability gets tight and prices can jump.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></details>
<!-- /wp:details -->

<!-- wp:details -->
<details class="wp-block-details"><summary>What is the weather like in Chicago in October?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Average highs run from about 69°F at the start of the month down to about 56°F by the end, with lows dropping from around 51°F to about 40°F. October averages about 3.4 inches of precipitation, so pack layers and a light rain layer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></details>
<!-- /wp:details -->

<!-- wp:details -->
<details class="wp-block-details"><summary>Does the Chicago Marathon affect hotel prices in October?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Yes, significantly. Chicago Marathon weekend in mid-October can make downtown hotel availability tight and drive prices up. Checking the exact date before booking, and shifting your trip by even a few days if the race isn't the point of your visit, can make a real difference in cost.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></details>
<!-- /wp:details -->

<!-- wp:details -->
<details class="wp-block-details"><summary>What events happen in Chicago in October?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
