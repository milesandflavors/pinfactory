<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>November in Chicago is really two different months stitched together. For the first three weeks, it feels like genuine off-season — thinner crowds, lower hotel rates, colder weather, and a city that is easier to do on a tighter budget.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then, around Thanksgiving, the mood changes fast: the Magnificent Mile lights up, Christkindlmarket usually opens at Daley Plaza, and downtown starts turning into the holiday city Chicago is known for. Knowing which version of November you're walking into changes how you should plan the trip.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="weather">Weather in Chicago in November — What to Pack</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>November is where Chicago stops being a fall city and starts being a winter one. National Weather Service climate normals for Chicago show average highs around 55°F and lows near 40°F on November 1, dropping to about 42°F and 29°F by November 30; the full-month average is 48°F / 34°F, with about 2.4 inches of precipitation and 1.8 inches of snow.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Wind off Lake Michigan regularly makes the actual feel colder than the number on the forecast, especially downtown and along the lakefront. This is a real coat month, not a jacket month — bring layers, a genuine winter coat by late November, gloves, and shoes that handle wet, cold pavement.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="early-vs-late">Early November vs. Late November</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Early to mid-November</strong> is the quietest stretch of Chicago's entire fall-into-winter calendar. The Halloween crowds are gone, the holiday crowds haven't arrived, and hotel rates typically sit at or near their lowest point of the season. It's a genuinely good window for museums, the Riverwalk on a crisp clear day, and sights that get crowded in summer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Late November</strong> — specifically the week of Thanksgiving — is a completely different city. The Magnificent Mile Lights Festival lands right around this point, Christkindlmarket usually starts around the same part of the calendar, Thanksgiving itself brings its own parade and family travel, and hotel demand rises to match. If you want to see Chicago's holiday season actually begin, this is the week; if you want the quiet, budget version of the month, avoid it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="events">Best Events in Chicago in November</h2>

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="hotels">Hotel Prices and Crowds</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Split the month in half when thinking about cost. Early to mid-November often runs below October's already-reasonable rates because it is past Marathon and Halloween demand and has not fully hit the holiday season yet.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Thanksgiving week changes that: parade attendance, family travel, the Lights Festival, and the expected start of Christkindlmarket season all land in the same handful of days, and pricing responds accordingly. If budget matters more than holiday atmosphere, book the first three weeks; if the holiday kickoff is the point, expect to pay more for it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="faq">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:details -->
<details class="wp-block-details"><summary>Is November a good time to visit Chicago?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It depends which week. Early to mid-November is quiet and generally the cheapest stretch of the fall calendar. Thanksgiving week is festive but busier and pricier, with the Lights Festival, Christkindlmarket's opening, and the Thanksgiving Parade all landing close together.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></details>
<!-- /wp:details -->

<!-- wp:details -->
<details class="wp-block-details"><summary>What is the weather like in Chicago in November?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Average highs drop from around 56°F early in the month to the low-to-mid 40s by the end, with lows in the 30s. Wind off Lake Michigan can make it feel colder than the forecast temperature. A real winter coat is appropriate by late November.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></details>
<!-- /wp:details -->

<!-- wp:details -->
<details class="wp-block-details"><summary>When does Christkindlmarket open in Chicago?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Christkindlmarket Chicago usually opens in late November and runs into Christmas Eve at Daley Plaza in the Loop, but the 2026 opening date should be confirmed on the official market calendar before you plan around it. The opening weekend usually falls in November even though most of the market's run is in December.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></details>
<!-- /wp:details -->

<!-- wp:details -->
<details class="wp-block-details"><summary>Are hotel prices higher during Thanksgiving week in Chicago?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Yes. Thanksgiving week combines parade attendance, family travel, the Lights Festival, and the expected start of Christkindlmarket season, which can raise both demand and pricing compared with earlier weeks in November.</p>
